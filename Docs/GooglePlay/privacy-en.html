PickaxeFall Privacy Policy

Document version: November 18, 2026. Developer: {{DEVELOPER}}. Contact: {{EMAIL}}.

This policy covers the Android Google Play edition of PickaxeFall. The Yandex Games browser edition uses that platform’s services. The separate offline APK does not include the advertising SDKs below.

Local play

Coins, resources, upgrades, unlocks, records, an unfinished run and settings are stored on your device. No developer account is required. We do not request contacts, precise location, camera or microphone access. You can reset progress in the game or clear the app’s data in Android settings.

Age and advertising

The age number is used to select a group and is not stored or sent by our code. Only the group and advertising choice are stored locally. Ad networks do not start before that choice. Users under 18 and users of unknown age use Kidoz child-appropriate contextual formats without interest-based advertising or remarketing. Adults use Yandex Mobile Ads and may allow personalization or decline it. All choices allow play. A changed age group applies after a full process restart; ads are disabled until then.

Advertising providers process technical request, device, app, impression and interaction data, IP addresses and other information described in their policies. IP addresses may reveal an approximate region even without location permission. AD_ID and location permissions are removed, and location consent is false. Declining personalization does not prevent technical processing needed for delivery and fraud prevention.

Play Games and purchases

If you choose to connect Play Games, Google processes your player identifier, achievements and progress selected for cloud storage. The unfinished run stays on your device. Resources are not combined across accounts. Copies may remain on your other devices. A permanent purchase to remove forced ads is handled by Google Play and Unity IAP. We do not receive your card number. A signed receipt is cached locally to verify and restore the entitlement. Google handles transaction information under its own terms.

Retention, deletion and contact

Local data remains until you reset or clear it. You can delete the cloud save from the game’s settings after signing in; synchronization stops until the next connection. Deletion does not cancel a purchase or delete Google’s transaction history or records held by advertising providers. See deletion instructions. Contact {{EMAIL}} for questions or requests. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

Third-party services may process information outside your country; their policies describe retention and handling. We do not operate our own analytics or account server. Available rights depend on your jurisdiction. This policy is updated when integrations change.
